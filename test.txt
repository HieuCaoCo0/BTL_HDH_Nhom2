BAO CAO BAI TAP LON HE DIEU HANH - NHOM 02

De tai: Cai dat va thu nghiem Haiku OS R1/beta5

Ung dung: Trinh soan thao van ban Haiku Notepad (BeAPI / C++)

KET QUA KIEM THU TINH NANG SOAN THAO VA HE THONG:

1. Soan thao van ban thuan tieng Viet chuan UTF-8 hoan chinh.

2. Co che Hoan tac / Lam lai nhieu buoc (Undo/Redo da cap):
- Ho tro toi da 1000 buoc hoan tac.
- Tu dong gom nhom thong minh theo tu va ngat dong.

3. Quan ly trang thai tai lieu:
- Tieu de cua so hien thi dau * khi co thay doi chua luu.
- Hop thoai SAlert canh bao xac nhan khi dong hoac mo file.

4. Tich hop sau Be File System (BFS):
- Tu dong gan thuoc tinh MIME "text/plain" khi luu file.
- Trinh duyet Tracker nhan dien bieu tuong va loai tep chuan.

5. Giao tiep huong su kien BMessage:
- Moi cua so BWindow van hanh tren mot luong doc lap.
- Khong bi khoa giao dien (freeze) khi thuc thi lenh I/O.
